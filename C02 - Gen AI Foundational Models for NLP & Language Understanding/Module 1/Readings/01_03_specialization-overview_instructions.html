The course “ Gen AI Model Foundations for NLP and Language Understanding” is the second course in the “ Generative AI Engineering with LLMs ” specialization. The specialization is designed to provide junior and mid-level professionals with a basic background in machine learning and neural networks an in-depth knowledge of using generative AI for Natural Language Processing (NLP). Participants will explore the principles, techniques, and applications of large language models (LLMs) in building NLP-based applications. Participants will utilize the PyTorch deep learning library for hands-on implementation. Through theoretical lectures, hands-on labs, and a final capstone project, participants will gain the skills and expertise to design and implement generative AI solutions using LLMs.

Specialization Content

The Generative AI Engineering Essentials with LLMs specialization comprises five short courses. Each course requires 3–6 hours of learner engagement time.

  • Course 1: Generative AI and LLMs: Architecture and Data Preparation

  • Course 2: Gen AI Foundational Models for NLP & Language Understanding

  • Course 3: Generative AI Language Modeling with Transformers

  • Course 4: AI Engineering with Transformer-Based LLMs

  • Course 5: Project: Generative AI Applications with RAG and LangChain

While you can take the courses individually, we suggest following the listed order for a better learning experience. For example, complete Course 1 before moving on to Course 2.
